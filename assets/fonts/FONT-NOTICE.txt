DeskPet Sans — bundled UI font subsets
======================================

Files:
  DeskPetSans-Regular.woff2   (weight 400)
  DeskPetSans-Semibold.woff2  (weight 600)

Modifications by the desk-pet project (2026-10-20):

  - subset to ASCII, GB2312, CJK punctuation and glyphs used by the app;
  - renamed the family to "DeskPet Sans" so the Reserved Font Name
    "Source" is not used by this Modified Version.
